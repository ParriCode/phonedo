How a deal closes

A paid 12-week pilot on one programme.

Scoping, week 0Write the policy in plain words, the reward per session and who funds the pool.
Setup, weeks 1–2One contract per programme, funded in USDC; sensors paired; two nurses trained.
Run, weeks 3–1220–50 patients. Sessions are verified and paid automatically.
Readout, week 12Adherence vs baseline, payouts made, zero raw data shared.
ConversionThe pilot price credits against an annual contract.
Agreed up front: completed sessions at least 15 points above baseline · 100% of payouts traceable on chain · no data-protection incident · nurse time per patient not higher than today. Expected cycle: 2–3 months to pilot, 6–9 months to an annual contract with an insurer.
