The solution03 / 09
What Phonendo does

Pay for the outcome.
Never touch the data.

1 · Measure

A sensor records one session: 32 readings of pulse or temperature.

2 · Prove

1 zero-knowledge proof: “the policy was met.” The data stays private.

3 · Get paid

A Soroban contract checks the proof and pays 0.1 USDC in seconds.

Cardiac rehab: 90–130 bpm in at least 28 of 32 readings
Cold chain: 2–8 °C in all 32 readings
